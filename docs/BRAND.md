# Biotérmica studio identity

Preserve the supplied emblem in `public/brand/biotermica-logo.webp`; do not redraw
or recolour it. Use it beside BIOTÉRMICA in the preview site's header. The product
cabinet remains unbranded, and the current renders contain no logo/text overlays.

- Navy `#073c52` for headings and identity.
- Aqua/deep blue `#009ecb` / `#007c9f` for water and interactive accents.
- Orange `#b94700` for heat cues.
- White and pale water-blue surfaces; pure white in product renders.
- Avenir Next, Avenir, Segoe UI, sans-serif, with a serif brand wordmark.

This is an internal technical studio, not a customer-facing website. Use concise
Spanish labels, file formats, dimensions and technical descriptions. Avoid slogans,
marketing copy, decorative collection numbers and introductory prose. Prefer
"Video", "Modelo 3D" and "Descargar GLB" to promotional wording. Keep errors
brief and actionable. Do not invent performance or engineering claims.
